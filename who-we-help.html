Who We Work With

We understand the business behind your projects.

Construction businesses face financial challenges that extend beyond traditional accounting. Project margins, work in progress, retentions, labour costs and cash flow all affect how your business performs.

Pallium works with established and growing construction businesses, combining specialist accounting knowledge with practical financial insight to help business owners make better commercial decisions.

Our Specialisations

Accounting and advisory for the way you operate.

Specialist financial support for four key areas of the construction industry.

01 / BUILDERS

Builders

We help builders understand project profitability, manage cash flow and maintain control over job costs. Get clearer financial information to support better decisions across current and future projects.

02 / CIVIL CONTRACTORS

Civil Contractors

We support civil contractors with financial visibility across complex contracts, equipment and labour costs. Better reporting and cash flow planning help you manage commitments and protect project margins.

03 / MAJOR TRADES

Major Trade Contractors

We help major trade contractors track job costs, labour expenses and contract profitability across multiple projects. Understand where margins are being made or lost and plan your cash flow with greater confidence.

04 / PROPERTY DEVELOPERS

Property Developers

We support property developers with development cost reporting, cash flow forecasting and financial planning. Gain clearer insight into project commitments and make informed decisions throughout the development lifecycle.
